How It Works

This is the simplest interesting Petri net: a cyclic state machine with three places and three transitions.

  • red → go → green: Light turns green
  • green → slow → yellow: Light turns yellow
  • yellow → stop → red: Light turns red

A single token circulates endlessly through the three places. The net is live (always has an enabled transition), bounded (each place holds at most 1 token), and deadlock-free.

This pattern — a token ring — appears in everything from traffic signals to round-robin schedulers.
